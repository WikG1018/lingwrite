# Trademarks and brand use

LingWrite's **source code** is licensed under the AGPL-3.0. The names "灵写" and "LingWrite", the app icon and related marks (the "Brand") are **not** covered by that license. The AGPL lets you modify and distribute the code. It does not give you the right to use the Brand.

## If you distribute a modified version, you must

1. **Rename it.** Don't call it "灵写" or "LingWrite", and don't use a name that could be confused with them, such as "LingWrite Plus".
2. **Replace the icon.** Don't use the LingWrite icon or anything clearly derived from it.
3. **Change the package name.** Your `applicationId` must not be `com.lingwrite.app` or start with `com.lingwrite.`. That way users won't mistake your build for the official one, and installs won't collide.
4. **Credit the source.** Saying "based on the LingWrite source code" is welcome. It's the attribution the AGPL expects, and it is fair use of the name.

## Uses that need no permission

- Sharing the unmodified official APK, with a note that it comes from this repository
- Mentioning or reviewing LingWrite in articles, reviews or tutorials
- Building the unmodified source for your own use, without distributing it

## Anything else

Using the Brand in a commercial product, an app store listing, merchandise or marketing requires prior written permission. Contact the maintainer @WikG1018 on GitHub.

The maintainer reserves all rights in the Brand.
